Cambridge B2 First (FCE) · Self-study

Someone finally marks your writing.

B2 First Tracker is a study system for preparing the Cambridge B2 First alone. It marks your writing against the four Cambridge bands, decides what to practise today, and shows the progress you cannot see on your own.

01What you get

Three things nobody gives you

A marker for your writing, a plan for today and a clear view of your progress — the parts of a course you lose when you study alone.

Writing marked, not vibes

Every text is scored on the four Cambridge bands from 0 to 5. Each score quotes the evidence from your own words, and every weak spot comes with three rewritten sentences you can actually reuse.

  • Four bands, 0–5
  • Evidence quoted from your text
  • Three rewrites per weak spot

A plan for today, not someday

It reads your practice log, finds the parts that are costing you marks and hands you today’s session: what to do, how long it takes and why it was chosen.

Progress you can see

Charts per part, per error type and per skill, plus your study streak and the countdown to exam day. The pattern behind a bad week stops being a mystery.

02Speaking

A real-time examiner

The four parts of the Speaking exam, in the browser, with the timings of the real test.

The speaking studio runs the four parts of the exam in the browser. You hear the examiner, answer out loud and get a transcript of the exchange when the time is up.

The report bands what Cambridge assesses: grammar and vocabulary, discourse management, pronunciation, and interactive communication. It is practice, not a certificate — the real examiner is still a person.

  • Parts 1 to 4, with their real timings
  • A partner voice for Part 3
  • A transcript and a banded report at the end

LimitationPronunciation is shown as not measured until the pronunciation service is configured on the server. The other scales work without it.

05Questions

Frequently asked questions

The short answers. The longer ones are in the privacy policy and the terms.

Is it really free?

Yes. The app is free to use and the source is MIT-licensed. If you use an AI key of your own, you pay your provider directly for what you use.

Do I need an email address?

No. An account is a username and a password. That is also why there is no email password link.

What if I forget my password?

Use the recovery request on the login screen. The operator resets the password by hand and answers at the contact you leave there; there is no email link.

Who sees my writing?

Only the AI provider used to mark it, and it is not stored.

Which parts of the exam does it cover?

Use of English and Reading, Writing, Listening, and Speaking.
