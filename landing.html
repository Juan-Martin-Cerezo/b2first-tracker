How it works

Three steps from diagnostic to exam day

No guesswork before you open a book, and no generic scores after you finish.

  1. 01

    It plans today's session from your diagnostic and your log.

    Take the 12-question baseline or start logging directly. The scheduler puts your weakest parts first and keeps all four papers rotating.

  2. 02

    You write or speak; the AI marks it against the Cambridge bands and quotes your own sentences.

    Submit a 140–190 word draft or sit a timed Speaking session to get 0–5 band scores, concrete weaknesses, and targeted rewrites.

  3. 03

    The plan adjusts tomorrow from what you actually did.

    Every logged session updates your progress by part, your error patterns, and the next unit in your queue automatically.

AI features

Focused where answer keys stop working

Four integrated tools for Writing, Speaking, fluency tracking, and adaptive daily planning.

01 / Writing corrector

Four Cambridge bands, quoted from your own text

Marks Essays, Articles, Reports, Reviews, and Emails from 0 to 5 on Content, Communicative Achievement, Organisation, and Language — with quoted sentences, concrete weaknesses, and three rewrites.

02 / AI Speaking examiner

All 4 parts, a listening partner, and your own tasks

Run one part or the full 14-minute test (2 / 4 / 4 / 4 min) with a partner role from Part 2 onward that reacts to what you say. Paste your own prompts anytime with Use my own task.

Practice, not a certificate: band estimates help you spot what to fix next; official B2 First grades come only from Cambridge examiners.

03 / Live Studio · Local VAD

Silences and speaking time, measured locally

Speak freely and see your speech ratio, pause count, and longest silence in real time using a local browser AudioWorklet (VAD). Nothing is recorded, and audio never leaves the tab.

04 / Adaptive daily plan

A daily plan derived from your log

A deterministic weekly rotation (By part or Full paper) built from your diagnostic and logged sessions, with official Cambridge timings and all 17 exam parts in one queue.

Privacy

Minimal data, transparent by design

Every boundary matches our public privacy policy — no hidden audio storage, no ad trackers, and full self-service control.

Audio never leaves the tab for Live Studio

Live Studio runs voice activity detection locally in your browser. In the live examiner, audio streams directly to Google via short-lived tokens and speaking audio is never written to disk.

API keys stay server-side

Shared provider keys never reach the browser. If you add your own provider key in Settings, it stays in your browser and is never saved on the server.

No ad trackers or profiling

No advertising networks, no tracking pixels, and no external font CDNs. The only web analytics is cookie-free aggregate Cloudflare Web Analytics (page views, country, and referrer).

No email required, full export & delete

Create an account with a username and 12-character password, Google sign-in, or a passkey. Export your full JSON history or permanently delete your account anytime.

FAQ

Direct answers

Exam format, AI limits, audio handling, custom tasks, and device support.

Is it really Cambridge format?

Yes. Writing uses the four published Cambridge B2 First assessment criteria (Content, Communicative Achievement, Organisation, and Language, 0–5 each) and the 140–190 word target. Speaking follows Parts 1–4 with official 2 / 4 / 4 / 4-minute timings, and all 17 exam parts are tracked in one log.

Does the AI replace a teacher?

No. The AI gives fast, structured practice feedback between lessons so you can fix recurring mistakes before exam day. Band estimates are not official Cambridge grades and do not replace a human teacher or examiner.

Do you store my audio?

No. Speaking audio is never written to disk. Live Studio runs voice activity detection locally in your browser so audio never leaves the tab; live voice streams directly via a short-lived token, and turn-based clips are dropped right after the request. Only audio files you explicitly upload under Listening → My own material are stored privately in your account.

Can I use my own tasks?

Yes. In Writing you can paste any exam prompt so Content is checked against every required point. In the Speaking exam you can paste your own prompts for any part with Use my own task, and in Listening you can upload your own audio, written questions, keys, and transcripts.

What does it cost?

It is free while in beta — one account, no card, and no email required. Shared AI features apply daily fair-use limits (5 Writing evaluations and 3 Speaking mocks / 45 min per UTC day), or you can use your own provider key in Settings.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Every view adapts to mobile screens, syncs your progress between phone and computer, and can be installed to your home screen. Microphone and speech recognition features depend on your mobile browser's Web Audio and speech support.
