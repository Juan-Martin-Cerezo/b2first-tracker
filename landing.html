Core AI & Adaptive Engine

Three parts of the exam you cannot grade with an answer key

Multiple-choice sheets tell you right from wrong. B2 First Tracker focuses AI strictly where self-study breaks down: Writing rubrics, real-time Speaking turns, and deciding what to study next.

01 / WRITING RUBRIC CORRECTOR

Scored on the four Cambridge bands, with evidence from your draft

Submit essays, articles, reports, reviews, or emails within the 140–190 word B2 First target. Paste the exam prompt so Content is checked against every point the question requires.

Instead of vague praise, the corrector scores each 0–5 band, quotes the lines that earned or cost marks, names two concrete weak spots, and provides three rewritten sentences you can study.

  • 4 named bands: Content, Communicative Achievement, Organisation, and Language
  • Quoted evidence: every score ties back to exact phrases from your text
  • Actionable output: 2 weak spots and 3 targeted sentence rewrites per draft
02 / VOICE EXAMINER · GEMINI LIVE

Four-part Speaking exam with real-time examiner and partner voices

Run a single part or the complete 14-minute B2 First Speaking sequence directly in your browser. Gemini Live drives natural voice turns with official exam clocks: 2 minutes for Part 1, then 4 minutes each for Parts 2, 3, and 4.

In Part 3, a partner voice collaborates with you on the task card and asks follow-up questions so you practise real turn-taking. When the clock ends, you receive a full transcript and a banded report.

  • Exact exam timings: Part 1 (2m), Part 2 (4m), Part 3 (4m), Part 4 (4m)
  • Partner voice in Part 3: interactive collaborative discussion and decision
  • Transcript & banded report: scored across Cambridge Speaking criteria

Practice, not a certificate: this voice examiner prepares you for timing and interaction under pressure. Official B2 First Speaking grades are always awarded by human Cambridge examiners.

03 / ADAPTIVE PLAN

A 12-item diagnostic turns weak parts into today's session

Start with a ten-minute baseline across Reading and Use of English parts 1 to 7. The tracker ranks your lowest-scoring parts and immediately places the weakest one at the front of your queue.

Every study day opens with a single prioritized task, its unit timer, and the exact reason it was selected. Behind it sits a weekly rotation that balances all four papers without letting you skip weak skills.

  • Weakest part first: diagnostic scores directly order your initial queue
  • One clear task per day: removes ten minutes of deciding what to open
  • Two rotation modes: by-part skill pairs or timed full-paper sittings

Under the hood

Engineered for judges to inspect, not trust blindly

Every security boundary, AI call, and scheduling rule is explicit in the repository. No hidden keys, no audio storage, and no hallucinated scores.

01 · Browser Requests live session

Never holds provider API keys; captures mic only inside /app.

02 · App Server Mints ephemeral token

Issues a short-lived single-use Gemini Live token; audio never touches disk.

03 · Gemini Live Direct voice stream

Streams examiner & partner turns directly; transcript feeds the banded report.

AUTH / EPHEMERAL

Server-minted Gemini Live tokens

The server mints short-lived ephemeral tokens for live voice sessions. Provider API keys stay strictly on the server and never reach the browser.

AUDIO / ZERO DISK

Audio is never written to disk

Speaking audio is streamed directly or dropped immediately after forwarding. It is never stored on disk, archived, or logged.

HONESTY / RUBRIC

Pronunciation: not measured

If speech pronunciation assessment is not configured on the server, the report explicitly shows not measured instead of guessing a band from text.

PLANNER / DETERMINISTIC

Deterministic weekly scheduler

Today's session and queue order are derived deterministically from your diagnostic and logged history—not regenerated by an LLM on refresh.

ZERO TRACKING

No third-party trackers or CDNs

Zero analytics SDKs, ad pixels, external font hosts, or third-party scripts. The public landing page ships as a single self-contained document.

PORTABILITY

JSON export & full deletion

Download your complete practice log and error history as JSON at any time, or permanently delete your account and server records in one action.

FAQ

Direct answers

What the AI scores mean, how accounts work, and how your data is handled.

Are Writing and Speaking bands official Cambridge scores?

No. They are practice estimates calibrated against the four published Cambridge B2 First assessment scales. Only Cambridge University Press & Assessment issues official exam certificates and grades.

Do I need an email address to create an account?

No. You can register with just a username and a password of at least 12 characters, or sign in with Google or a passkey.

What happens when I delete my account?

Deleting your account from the Account panel permanently removes your user record, sessions, progress history, and error log from the server. Export a JSON copy first if you want to keep your history.

How is AI used across the platform?

AI powers two practice loops: marking Writing drafts on the four Cambridge bands (with quoted evidence, two weak spots, and three rewrites) and running the 4-part Speaking voice examiner via Gemini Live ephemeral tokens. Your study plan, queue rotation, and stage gates are deterministic.
