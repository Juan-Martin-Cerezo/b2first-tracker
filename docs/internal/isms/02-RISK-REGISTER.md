# 02 — Risk Register

Method: likelihood and impact are scored 1–5 by the single owner; the score is
likelihood × impact. A control is only listed as **existing** when it was read in the code
and, where available, exercised by a test. Items that could not be verified are marked
`unverified` and carry the worst credible impact. Residual scores are the owner's estimate
after the existing control, not a measurement.

| Score | 1–4 | 5–9 | 10–15 | 16–25 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Band | Low | Medium | High | Critical |

Review cadence: quarterly for Medium and above, annually for Low. Owner: the single instance
operator. No risk has an independent reviewer.

## Accepted risks (documented, not fixed)

- `'unsafe-inline'` in CSP (R02/R03) — accepted until the UI refactor.
- Password minimum 10 and 1,000-entry blocklist (R16) — a deliberate product constraint,
  explicitly marked FAIL in `docs/SECURITY-CHECKLIST.md`.
- No MFA enforcement (R17) — passkeys are available but optional.
- Host root trust (R12) — no disk encryption, stated in `SECURITY.md §5.3`.
