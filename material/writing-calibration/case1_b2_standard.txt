Nowadays, the environment is a big problem in the world. Some people think individuals have to protect it, while others say it is the government's job.

First of all, people can do many things at home. They can recycle plastic and glass, and also try to produce less rubbish every day. If everybody does this, the planet will be cleaner.

On the other hand, governments are very important because they can make laws. For example, they can stop big factories from putting smoke in the air. Without laws, many companies will not care about nature and will continue polluting.

Also, I think technology can help a lot. Scientists can invent electric cars and better energy so we don't use oil anymore.

In conclusion, both people and governments need to work together, but in my opinion governments should do more because they have the money and power to change things.
