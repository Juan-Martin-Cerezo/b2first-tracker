Nowadays, protecting the environment is a very important topic all over the world, and many people have different opinions about who should take responsibility for protecting nature. Some people believe that ordinary individuals should take responsibility for the environment, while other people believe that national governments should be the ones who take responsibility for solving environmental problems in our world today.

First of all, individuals can help protect the environment by recycling and producing less household waste every day. For example, people can separate plastic bottles, glass jars, and paper at home so that this waste can be recycled instead of being thrown away. In addition, people can buy less plastic packaging when they go shopping at the supermarket, which also reduces the total amount of rubbish that each family creates. In other words, when ordinary people recycle more and throw away less rubbish at home, they directly reduce waste and help keep the environment cleaner.

On the other hand, governments also have a very important role because they are able to create laws and regulations to protect the environment. Without strong environmental laws, many large factories and companies would continue polluting the air and rivers simply because polluting is cheaper for them. Therefore, governments must introduce strict regulations and punish companies that break those environmental rules. By passing clear laws and making sure businesses obey them, governments can control pollution on a much larger scale than individual people can.

Also, education is another important idea that can help protect the environment in the future. Schools can teach children about climate change and explain why protecting nature is necessary for everyone. When young students learn good habits at school, they remember these lessons and continue caring about the environment as adults.

In conclusion, even though individuals can recycle and reduce waste at home and schools can teach young people about nature, I believe governments should take the main responsibility because strict laws and regulations create the biggest change for the whole planet.
