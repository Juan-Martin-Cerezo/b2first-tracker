Protecting the environment is a major global concern today and there is debate about whether individuals or governments should take responsibility. Citizens can recycle their household waste and reduce the amount of plastic packaging they buy every week. Reducing personal waste helps decrease the volume of garbage in landfills. Governments have the authority to implement strict environmental laws and regulations. Authorities can penalise factories that release toxic chemicals into rivers and oceans. Subsidising renewable energy sources like wind and solar power is another effective measure that states can take. Education plays a fundamental role in shaping future attitudes towards nature. Teaching students about climate change in schools encourages sustainable habits from an early age. Both ordinary people and political leaders have duties to fulfil. Governments should ultimately take the lead because national legislation creates a much broader impact than isolated personal efforts.
